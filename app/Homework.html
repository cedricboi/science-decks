How it works

  1. Open learnwithmrcedric, sign in with your own PIN, and tap Homework.
  2. Tap Start to open a worksheet. Write your answers on it with the Apple Pencil.
  3. When you have finished, tap Hand in. After your teacher marks it, tap See marked work to see the marking.
  4. When your teacher gives out the answers, tap See answers and check your corrections against them.
