How a Coach works

  1. 1In Coaches, choose Download the Coach.
  2. 2Open it from your Downloads, then sign in inside it once, with your class, name and password.
  3. 3Get the Excel file you need from Task files, and work on it in Excel.
  4. 4Drop your file into the Coach's file checker. It tells you what is right, and keeps a copy in My files.
