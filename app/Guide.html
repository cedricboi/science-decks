Before you start

  • Your MOE Google account (moe.edu.sg)
  • A computer, for the setup steps
  • The seven script files, Code.gs, Index.html, Teacher.html, Rewards.html, Styles.html, Ink.html and Guide.html, from the colleague who shared this guide

What you will end up with

Students sign in once, in learnwithmrcedric, with their own PIN

With Live Board linked, homework is the Homework tab of learnwithmrcedric. Students choose their class, then type their own name and their 4-digit PIN. The iPad stays signed in. There is no sign-in on this page any more.

PART 1Create the Sheet and the script

learnwithmrcedric is one app now

Live Board and the Teacher Hub share one script, in the Live Board sheet, with one web address. The Teacher Hub sheet and its Drive folders stay as they are. To set it up or move to it, follow the code page (Move, paste, setupMyScience, deploy). The steps below describe the Hub sheet itself.

  1. In Google Drive, signed in with your moe.edu.sg account, create a new Google Sheet. Name it Worksheet Hub.
  2. In the Sheet, open Extensions > Apps Script. A new browser tab opens.
  3. At the top left, rename the project from "Untitled project" to Worksheet Hub.
  4. On the left there is a file called Code.gs. Click it, delete everything in it, and paste in the whole of the Code.gs file.
  5. Click the + beside "Files", choose HTML, and name it Index. Do not type ".html", because Apps Script adds it. Delete everything in the new file and paste in the whole of the Index.html file.
  6. Do the same five more times: files named Teacher, Rewards, Styles, Ink and Guide, with the whole of Teacher.html, Rewards.html, Styles.html, Ink.html and Guide.html pasted in. Guide is this guide, shown on the Teacher Hub's sign-in page.
  7. Press Ctrl + S (Cmd + S on a Mac) to save.
  8. Optional: set the time zone to (GMT+08:00) Singapore under the gear icon, and change APP_TITLE and APP_SCHOOL near the top of Code.gs for the students' sign-in page.

PART 3Add students and a worksheet

Students

    • Reg No is what a student types the first time they sign in, to show it is them. Without one, you let them in from your phone.
    • Code fills itself in (S001, S002 …), one for each student, never given twice. Handed-in work and Claude use the class and the code, never the name. Leave the codes as they are.
    • Write the class the same way every time, for example 1E2. Upper or lower case and spaces do not matter.
  2. Click Worksheet Hub > 2. Check student list. It shows how many students are in each class. It also warns you if two students in the same class have exactly the same name. If they do, add something to tell them apart, such as an initial.

PART 4Publish the student page

  1. In the Apps Script tab, click Deploy > New deployment.
  2. Click the gear beside "Select type" and choose Web app.
  3. Fill in:
    • Description: Worksheet Hub
    • Execute as: Me
    • Who has access: Anyone (see the table below)
  4. Click Deploy, then copy the Web app URL. It ends in /exec. This is the one link students use for every worksheet.

PART 5Test it before your class does

  1. Test it yourself. Open the /exec link in Safari on an iPad. Choose class TEST, press First time here?, type the name Test Student and register number 99, and choose a PIN. The TEST student sees every open worksheet. Open one, write on it with the Apple Pencil, rest your palm on the screen while you write, scroll with a finger, and tap Hand in.
  2. Test one student iPad. Open the same link on a student's iPad in Safari. This is the real test, because school iPads can have extra restrictions. If anything stops it, such as a block page, a Google sign-in loop or a blank page, take a photo of the screen for your ICT team.
  3. Check the Submissions tab. Each hand-in appears there with the time, the number of pages written on, and a link to the PDF.

PART 6Put the link on your Google Site

On your class page, add a Button (Insert > Button) that links to the /exec URL, with a label such as "Open my worksheets". A button is better than embedding the page. On an iPad, the page then opens full screen, which gives students more room to write.

One link for the whole year

You never need a new link for a new worksheet. Publish it in the Teacher Hub, and it appears for students straight away.

PART 7Open the Teacher Hub

The Teacher Hub is where you do everything after setup. It is the student link with ?teacher added at the end.

  1. In the Sheet, choose Worksheet Hub > Set teacher passcode and type a passcode of at least 6 characters. Students must not know it.
  2. Choose Worksheet Hub > Open the Teacher Hub and open the link. Bookmark it on your laptop and iPad.
  3. If you are signed in to Chrome or Safari with your moe.edu.sg account, the Hub opens straight away. Otherwise type the passcode. You stay signed in for 6 hours. Read the setup guide on the sign-in page opens this guide.

Needs you lists what to do next, grouped, each with one button. The moon puts an item away until tomorrow. Each worksheet card has a ring per class (late work in amber) and one button for the next step.

What the Hub does for you

  • New worksheet: choose the PDF, the classes and the due date, then Publish
  • Mark with Claude: marking, scores and answer key straight into the Hub
  • Download zip: every hand-in for that worksheet in one file
  • Tracker: who is late or missing, class by class

Links along the top

  • Student page, to check what students see
  • Class site, your Google Site
  • Sheet and Drive folder, if you ever need them

PART 8Connect Claude marking (once)

After this, one button in the Hub asks Claude to mark a worksheet. Claude fetches the hand-ins, marks them against your marked solution and puts everything back in the Hub. You do not upload or download anything.

  1. Deploy the new version: Deploy > Manage deployments > Edit > Version: New version > Deploy. The link stays the same.
  2. In the Sheet, check Worksheet Hub > Set the web app link holds the link that ends in /exec. Claude uses it to reach the Hub.
  3. Open claude.ai/code/routines and open Worksheet Hub marking. Choose Add an API trigger, then Generate token. The token is shown once, so copy it straight away.
  4. In the Teacher Hub, tap Claude marking at the top. Paste the trigger URL and the token, then tap Save.

What the token can do

It lets the Hub start the marking run and nothing else. If it is ever lost, generate a new one in the same place and paste it in again.

Mark with Claude on a worksheet. Leave Check one sample first ticked. The note tells Claude anything your solution does not show.

A worksheet from start to finish

  1. Publish. In the Hub, tap New worksheet. Choose the PDF and your marked solution (or tick Let Claude draft it now), tick the classes, set the due date and tap Publish. Tick Hand in saved work by itself and Start Claude's marking when it closes to let the Hub do the rest on time (next pages).
  2. Mark. Claude starts by itself at the due time, or tap Mark with Claude when Needs you says the worksheet is ready. A worksheet from your library that Claude marked for another class, which you released, is marked all at once with no sample. Otherwise, a few minutes later the card says Sample marked. Open the sample and the answer key. Tap Mark the rest, or type what to change and tap Mark the sample again with my note.
  3. Check. Marked work appears in the marks table batch by batch. When the card says Claude has finished marking, read Please check; Check the marking opens the scripts Claude asked about first.
  4. Release. Tap Release to students. Each student sees their marked copy, score and comment, and can hand in corrections in green. Nothing is visible to them before this.
  5. Answers. Tap Release answers when corrections are done, or let the Hub release them when corrections close.

The worksheet page after the sample. Late hand-ins are marked the same way, and Claude marks only the ones not marked yet. You can still tap Download zip and mark in a chat with Claude (/markforge) or in any PDF app, then bring the files back with Return marked work.

Check and change the marking

Open any script before you release it, on your PC or iPad. Claude's ticks and crosses sit on top of the student's work as separate marks, so any of them can be erased, and you can add your own.

The checking screen. The marks panel on the right sets each question's mark, and the margin marks and circled total follow it.

  1. Open. On the worksheet page, tap Check marking to start at the first script you have not checked, or Check on any row of the marks table.
  2. Change. Use Tick, Cross, Pen and Text to add marks and the Eraser to remove any mark. Change a question's mark with + and −, and edit the feedback and comment. Undo takes back any step.
  3. Save. Save and next rebuilds the marked PDF, saves the score and comment, and opens the next student.

On an iPad

  • The Apple Pencil draws and a finger scrolls
  • A finger tap still places a tick or cross
  • No Pencil? Turn on Finger draws
  • The Marks button opens the marks panel

Good to know

  • A saved script shows Checked, and Claude never changes it again
  • Change the sample, then Mark the rest: Claude follows your changes
  • Scripts Claude has not marked open for marking by hand

Your worksheet library in Drive

Keep every worksheet in one Drive folder. Each gets a folder of its own, and after marking it also holds the marked solution, the answer key and Claude's notes, so the next time you set it, everything comes with it.

In New worksheet, tap From my library, pick a worksheet, then choose the classes and due date and Publish.

Fill the library

  • Drop worksheets into Worksheet library in your Drive, in topic folders if you like, with any marked solution named like the worksheet plus solution
  • Tap Refresh: each worksheet gets a folder of its own
  • Or tick Keep it in my library when you upload, or use Save to my library in a worksheet's ··· menu
  • PDFs, Google Docs and Google Slides. Save Word files as PDF first

Kept up to date

  • Your saved marked solution, Claude's answer key and Claude's notes go into the worksheet's folder
  • Set it again: all three come with it, the answer key hidden until you release it
  • Set before shows the classes and due date, so no class gets it twice
  • A new marked solution drops the old notes, so Claude works from the new one

Moving marks and writing

Ticks, crosses and writing can be picked up and moved, by you on the checking screen and by students on their worksheet.

Checking screen. With Tick or Cross, press on a tick or cross that is already there (Claude's or yours) and drag it into place. A tap still places a new one. Move (the four arrows, key M) drags anything: ticks, crosses, pen writing and text. With Text, drag text to move it or tap it to change or delete it. Undo takes back every move.

Students. The lasso button selects writing: draw a loop round it, then drag to move it, drag the round corner to resize it, or tap Copy or Delete.

Writing selected on a student's iPad.

Big, clear marks

Claude's ticks and crosses, and the ones you add, are drawn big and bold so it is obvious the work has been marked. In the margin beside each question there is one tick for full marks or one cross if any mark was lost, with the mark beside it. There is never a half tick.

Hands-off homework

How it runs in New worksheet, Edit and Copy to classes is one choice. Hands-off (the usual) hands in and starts marking by itself at the due time; you check and release once, corrections close a week later and then the answers show. I mark it myself leaves Claude out. Stays open takes late work. Advanced has the separate switches below.

Hand in saved work by itself

  • At the due time, anyone who wrote on the worksheet but did not tap Hand in has their saved work handed in, on time
  • An iPad with the worksheet open hands it in a minute before
  • The others are made into PDFs by the Hub the next time you open it, marked Auto in the marks table
  • A blank worksheet is never handed in

Start Claude's marking when it closes

  • Claude marks its sample by itself, so it is waiting for you to check
  • It waits for your marked solution if there is none yet. Needs you reminds you
  • A timer every 15 minutes does this. The Hub sets it up the first time you tick a box

Let Claude draft your marked solution

  1. Tick Let Claude draft it now in New worksheet, or tap Let Claude draft the marked solution in the Set step.
  2. When Needs you says it is ready, tap Check it. Claude's answers are in blue with B1, M1 and A1 in red. Drawings are worked out from the worksheet's own lines and measured.
  3. Erase or change anything, add other accepted answers, then tap Save as marked solution. Claude marks against what you save. Students never see it.

Copy a worksheet to other classes or next year

Tap the copy button beside a worksheet's title (or Copy to classes in the ··· menu). Tick the classes and give each its own due date. Your marked solution, answer key, picture, hints, formula card and notes come too. Classes with the same due date share one copy, and nothing students did is copied.

On the iPads, each open worksheet is kept on the iPad, so it opens at once.

Homework tracker

The tracker checks every student in your Students tab against every worksheet, and highlights who hands in late or not at all.

An example Tracker tab. The small orange corner on a cell holds the time the student first handed in.

Making and updating it

  1. The Tracker tab in the Teacher Hub is always up to date, with cards for students to follow up and each student's record a tap away. The Sheet copy updates itself after you save marks. To update it by hand, click Worksheet Hub > Update homework tracker. It makes one tab per class, called Tracker 1E2 and so on.
  2. Run it again at any time to bring it up to date. The tab is rebuilt each time, so do not type in it.
  3. To keep it up to date without clicking, choose Update the tracker every hour (turn on or off). Choose it again to turn this off.

What each cell means

  • ✓ handed in on time, followed by the score if you have typed one
  • Late 2d handed in 2 days after the due time (or Late 5h for 5 hours)
  • ✗ not handed in, and the due time has passed or the worksheet is closed
  • Grey not handed in yet, and not due yet

On time or late is decided by the first time a student hands in, so handing in again later does not make earlier work late.

Who is flagged, and when to message a parent

  • A problem is a worksheet missing, late, or under 50% once you release the marking
  • 1 or 2 problems: amber, on the follow-up list with what they are
  • 3 problems: red, Message parent (3). The Hub writes the message, listing them
  • Tap Parent messaged once it is sent. The count starts again from that day

The student's name is shaded in the same colour. Only worksheets that are already due count.

Points, avatars and the night market

Students earn points for each worksheet, spend them on clothes for a 3D avatar, and change them into tokens for real prizes at a night market at the end of term. While a class is in its Science lesson, the avatar and the night market are closed to that class. Turn on Avatars and night market open on your phone to open them.

My avatar
Opened from the avatar's head or My avatar at the top of the homework page. Tap to try things on, then buy them with points.
Night market
A square in an HDB estate with Marina Bay behind. The two-sided leaderboard stands in the middle, with initials (and You for the student's own row).

Running the night market

  • In the Hub, open Points. Tap a prize card to change it
  • Switch the market Open at the end of term. Before that, students can look but not buy
  • A student who gets a prize is given a four-letter code. Check it and tap Handed over
  • Tap + beside a student to give or take points, or use a quick bonus
  • Mostly blank in the marks table is ticked by Claude's marking, and you can change it
  • The leaderboard shows the top ten point earners by their initials. Show each class, all classes or hide it
  • Stickers (1 to 3 tokens) show in a speech bubble, one every 8 seconds

Every purchase, token change and prize is also a row in the Rewards log tab of the Sheet. To see what students see, tap See it as a student.

First lesson tip

Show the Help guide on the projector, then set a one-page practice worksheet so students try the tools before a real one.

My folders and notes

At the bottom of the sky at the top of the homework page, students switch between To do and My folders. To do is the list of worksheets, newest first. In My folders, Find a worksheet or note sits at the top of the folders.

  • Folders: in My folders, students make folders with a name and a colour. Tapping ⋯ on a worksheet gives it their own name, moves it to a folder or colours it. You always see the real title.
  • Notes: + New note makes a note on lined, squared, graph or blank paper, to write on with the same pens and tools. Up to 3 notes of up to 20 pages each. Save as PDF opens the iPad's share sheet.
  • You can read every note: in the Tracker, click a student. Their notes are at the bottom of their panel, with View and PDF.

Writing on the worksheet

The student's writing is in blue. The worksheet itself cannot be changed.
  • Pens and toolsSix pen colours in four sizes, a highlighter, straight and dashed lines, arrows, rectangles and ovals, and typed text with keys for Ω, °C, µ and powers of ten. Holding the Pencil still at the end of a line makes it straight. Green is kept for corrections.
  • ToolkitA row of tools above the worksheet: ruler, protractor, normal, stamps, graph helper, calculator, read aloud, I'm stuck and How sure. See the next page.
  • Apple PencilThe Pencil writes and a finger scrolls, so a resting palm leaves no marks.
  • No PencilThe finger writes. The "Finger writes / Finger scrolls" button switches between the two.
  • Eraser, undo and redoThe eraser removes whole strokes, shapes or text. Undo and redo step through changes.
  • ZoomFrom 40% to 250% with the zoom buttons. Tap the percentage to go back to 100%.
  • Extra pageAdds a blank page at the end for working.
  • SavingAutomatic every 30 seconds and when the student taps Back. If the iPad goes offline, the writing is kept on the iPad and sent later.
  • Hand inStudents can hand in again until you untick Open. Only the latest copy is kept.
  • CorrectionsAfter you release the marking, students tap Do corrections, write in green on their marked copy and hand it in. You track this on the worksheet page.

Lessons from the Hub

With Live Board linked, the Teacher Hub is where a lesson starts, and your phone runs it.

  1. Three tabs. Home has Today (Needs you, This week and Teach next) and Lessons. Needs you shows only what needs doing; due dates, corrections and students to keep an eye on are in This week, a card on Home. Homework has Worksheets and the Tracker. Students has Progress and Classes. Points and This week are in the menu (T). Live Board is in Links.
  2. Teach next. On Home, each class has a card with its next lesson (the next part after the one taught last, or the next lesson in the subject). Press Teach: the slides open for the projector, already signed in for that class and part. Nothing is asked. A lesson already running shows Open the slides.
  3. Lessons (on Home). Every lesson on the lesson site. Choose the class at the top, then Teach (lessons with parts ask which part, the next one marked). Add or update a lesson and Pair my phone are here too.
  4. The projector screen. On the laptop you take to class, press Projector screen (Teach next). It pairs itself for a year and waits, showing the class on now. Choose the lesson on your phone and it opens the slides by itself; End on the phone brings it back to waiting. You can also tap a class on the screen to teach from the laptop.
  5. Your phone. Pair it once (Pair my phone, scan, press Allow); it stays paired for a year. With no lesson running it shows Teach next (the class on now first) with Teach on the projector, and Needs you with a button for each thing (Release marking, Let in, Given, Mark with Claude). During a lesson it opens on that lesson, with every student's name in colour (green with you, orange behind, blue ahead, red off the slides, dashed not in yet; tap a name for their slide), checked every 3 seconds, Pause, Follow and End at the bottom, and Show answers under each question. A student who goes past your slide sees You are ahead of your teacher on their iPad. The class name at the top opens the lesson's settings (with Avatars and night market open) and A student's sign-in (Sign out, Reset PIN). Let in shows when a student setting up a PIN has no register number, or typed it wrong three times.
  6. Homework for each sub-chapter. On Lessons, Homework beside a lesson lists its sub-chapters. Link a worksheet from your library to each one, or let them link themselves: a worksheet named with the sub-chapter's number (3.2 Heat capacity.pdf, or inside a 3.2 … folder) is used for 3.2 with no linking. Choose No homework to stop that for a sub-chapter. From release 25, each chapter in Lessons opens a page of its own: Teach for each lesson, and under it the homework linked to each sub-chapter, showing only its next step (Add, Deploy, then Mark), with the rest under its ⋯ button. Deploy is one tap, due at the class's next lesson, with Change date and Undo on the note after it. A new worksheet can go straight into a chapter: in the Lesson library, Upload a worksheet puts the PDF (and its marked solution) into your worksheet library, in a folder for the chapter, as a topical worksheet or as one part's homework. Nothing reaches a class until you deploy it: in the chapter's package on Lessons, each worksheet waits (dashed) with Deploy. Once at least 70% of a sub-chapter's slides, including its last slide, have been on the projector for a class (each for 8 seconds or more, over one lesson or several), its worksheet shows Taught · ready, and Deploy offers the start of their next lesson as the due date, hands-off. Paging quickly through the slides does not count. Each deployed worksheet then shows Deployed, Marking, and Completed once you release the marking; it stays in the students' package.
  7. What runs by itself. Starting a lesson for one class ends any lesson still running for another (you have moved rooms). A lesson on the class's timetable lasts its usual length even while the laptop sleeps; other lessons end 10 minutes after the projector slides close (open them again within the hour and it carries on, with Follow me and Keep answers hidden as they were). Answers show when you step on past a question's slide, or once everyone has answered; turn on Keep answers hidden under More on your phone to stop this. The Hub learns each class's timetable from when lessons start, for the due dates.
  8. Revision. A lesson opens for revision by itself once you teach it to a class. On Lessons, the Revision switch closes it (or opens it) for the class chosen at the top. Students see it under Revise in learnwithmrcedric and can go through it after school. Nothing they do there is saved or counted. Answers count only while you are teaching that lesson to their class.
  9. Progress and Classes. Live Board's pages, as tabs in the Hub. Names and scores stay hidden until you show them.
  10. Results. On the slides, Results (bottom left) shows how the class answered the questions on the slide, with no names. The right answer shows only when you press Show the right answer.

Linking the Hub and Live Board

The Teacher Hub and Live Board are one app now. Live Board's sheet, Settings tab, Class list sheet ID is the Hub sheet's ID (the long part of its address): that is how the app finds the Hub sheet. Menu (T) > Setup check and updates shows anything missing, with a button to fix it.

Student sign-in

Each student has a 4-digit PIN they choose, and a security question they choose from a list. Live Board keeps only a scrambled copy of each, never the PIN or the answer itself.

  1. Signing in. The student chooses their class and types their own name and PIN. There is no list of names anywhere: the name is checked against your Students tab (capitals, spaces and word order do not matter). A wrong name and a wrong PIN get the same answer, so nobody can find out who is in a class.
  2. The first time. In learnwithmrcedric the student presses First time here?, types their name and register number (from the Students tab), then chooses a PIN and a security question and types the answer. A student with no register number, or who types it wrong three times, presses Ask my teacher to let me in, and you press Let in on your phone or in Needs you. During the class's own Science lesson no register number is needed: you are in the room. Your phone shows who set up a PIN that way (new PINs), and Reset PIN undoes a wrong one.
  3. After that. They type their PIN. After 5 wrong PINs they wait 15 minutes. After 15 wrong PINs only you can unlock them.
  4. Forgot my PIN. They choose the security question they picked, answer it and choose a new PIN. Every other iPad signed in as them is signed out.
  5. The name stays on the iPad. Once signed in, learnwithmrcedric and the slides stay signed in as that student. Only you can change it: your PIN on the iPad (Not you? in learnwithmrcedric), or Sign out on your phone or in Classes.
  6. Reset PIN (phone or Classes) clears the PIN and the question. The student sets them up again with their register number.

Keep your own PIN long

One teacher PIN signs in the Teacher Hub, Live Board, your phone and the projector screen, and decides who can change any student's name or PIN. Use at least 6 numbers (Live Board sheet, Settings tab, Teacher PIN). After 20 wrong tries it stops for 15 minutes. Paired phones and laptops keep a key for a year; changing the PIN unpairs them all.

Updates

  1. When an update is ready, Home says so. Menu (T) > Setup check and updates > Update puts it in, in about a minute, and the web address stays the same. Undo the update puts the version before back.
  2. The first time, Google asks you to switch on the Apps Script API for your account: open script.google.com/home/usersettings and switch on Google Apps Script API.
  3. By hand instead: paste each new file over the old one in the Live Board sheet's script (Code, Hub, App and the HTML files), then Deploy > Manage deployments, the pencil, New version, Deploy. Do not use "New deployment", because that makes a new link.

Limits to know about

  • There is no password. A student can choose a classmate's name and open, change or hand in that classmate's worksheet, and see their marked work. The Submissions tab records the time of every hand-in, which helps if you need to check.
  • Google limits how many students can save at exactly the same moment (about 30). The page tries again automatically, so a busy moment only delays saving by a few seconds.
  • Worksheet PDFs larger than about 15 MB load slowly. The Check column warns you.
  • All student work is stored in the Drive of the teacher who set up the Sheet. If you leave the school or delete the folder, the work goes with it.
