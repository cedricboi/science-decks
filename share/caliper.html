How to measure

  1. Close the jaws fully. The display should read 0.00 mm. If it does not, press ZERO.
  2. Choose an object. Use the outside jaws for an outer diameter, the inside jaws for an internal diameter and the depth rod for a depth.
  3. Move the jaw until it touches the object. Record the display reading to 2 decimal places.

How to measure

  1. Close the spindle with the ratchet. The display should read 0.000 mm. If it does not, press ZERO.
  2. Choose an object. The spindle opens wider than the object.
  3. Turn the thimble until the spindle is close to the object. Finish with the ratchet. Turning the thimble too far squeezes the object and gives a reading that is too small.
  4. Record the display reading to 3 decimal places. Turn the display off to practise reading the sleeve and thimble scales.
